How it works
LIVE
  1. 1
    Shop prints the QR poster
    Generated automatically in the owner console, unique per shop.
  2. 2
    Customer scans & uploads
    No install, no signup. Word files are previewed as PDF pages.
  3. 3
    Owner verifies payment
    See the UPI reference, then Verify & Print or Reject.
  4. 4
    Local agent prints it
    A tiny agent on the shop PC picks the job and sends it to your printer.
📱
